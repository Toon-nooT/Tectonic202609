A question becomes knowledge.

The explanation stays connected to its sources, its scope and the person who confirmed it.

01 / FIND

Compare the sources.

See the conflicting passages together, with their original documents. A mismatch is a question to investigate.

02 / CAPTURE

Ask the person who knows.

An optional voice conversation captures the missing distinction, exception or effective date in the expert’s own words.

03 / CONFIRM

Get the wording right.

The assistant reads the clarification back. The expert corrects it or explicitly confirms it before anything is saved.

04 / REUSE

Help the next colleague.

Search for the confirmed explanation, see who contributed it and inspect the supporting sources in one place.

Expert-confirmed, with context. Original sources are preserved. A clarification is attributed guidance for its stated scope.
