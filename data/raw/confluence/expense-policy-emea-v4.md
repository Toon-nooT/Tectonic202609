# Expense Policy EMEA (v4)

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all employees and contractors in EMEA entities submitting business expense claims through the expense portal.

## 2. Eligible Expenses

| Category | Limit | Receipt required |
|----------|-------|------------------|
| Meals (business) | EUR 35 / person | Yes |
| Hotel | EUR 180 / night | Yes |
| Taxi / ride-share | Reasonable | Yes above EUR 25 |
| Mobile data roaming | Actual | Yes |

## 3. Submission

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims older than 30 days require approval from the department head.

## 4. Reimbursement Timeline

Claims are reimbursed within 10 business days after approval by the line manager.

Approved claims are paid in the next weekly payment run. Payment runs execute every Thursday.

## 5. Exceptions and Escalation

For questions or disputes, contact the Finance Process Manager via the `#finance-ops` channel.
