# Belgium: Public Holiday Carry-Over

**Article ID:** KB0012874
**Category:** Leave & Absences / Belgium
**Author:** Employee Support
**Published:** 2024-12-05
**Last updated:** 2025-12-18
**Audience:** Frontline agents

## Summary

How to advise clients on carrying over unused public holiday balances into the next calendar year.

## When does this apply?

The employee has a remaining public holiday balance (replacement days) at year end and the client asks whether it can be used in the following year.

## Answer

Unused public holiday can be carried over until March 31.

After that date the balance expires, unless a sectoral agreement states otherwise.

## Steps for agents

1. Confirm the employee's paritair comité.
2. Check the remaining balance in the leave module.
3. Inform the client of the carry-over deadline.
4. If the client disputes the deadline, escalate to the Senior Payroll Lead.
