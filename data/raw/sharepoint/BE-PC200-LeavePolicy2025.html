Paritair Comité 200 - Leave & Working Time Policy 2025

Version 3.2 | Effective 2025-01-01 | Owner: Payroll Policy

Leave | Working time | Overtime | Appendix

1. Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 20 statutory leave days per calendar year. Part-time employees accrue leave pro rata to their contractual hours.

Leave requests must be submitted at least 14 calendar days in advance, except in cases of force majeure.

2. Working Time

The standard working week is 38 hours. A standard working day is 7.6 hours, averaged over the reference period.

  • Maximum daily working time: 9 hours
  • Maximum weekly working time: 48 hours
  • Minimum daily rest period: 11 consecutive hours

3. Overtime Compensation

Overtime must be pre-approved by the line manager and recorded in the time-tracking system on the same day.

ConditionRate
Weekday overtime150% rate applies after 8 hours worked.
Sunday / public holiday200%

Note: Overtime rates are calculated on the gross hourly base wage and exclude shift premiums.
